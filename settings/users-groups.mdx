Your Document360 portal is a collaborative space where writers, editors, and administrators work together to build and maintain your knowledge base. Users & groups lets you invite team members, organize them for easier permission management, and revoke access when needed — all from one place.

## Inviting team members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Users & groups">
    Go to **Settings** → **Users & permissions** → **Users & groups**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite a new user">
    Click **Invite user** and enter the team member's email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a role">
    Select a role from the dropdown. The role determines what actions the user can perform in the portal. See [Roles & Permissions](/settings/roles-permissions) for a full breakdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Click **Send invite**. The team member receives an email with a link to accept the invitation and set up their account. The invitation link expires after 72 hours — you can resend it from the users list if needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## User roles

Roles define the boundaries of what a portal user can see and do. Document360 includes the following built-in roles:

- **Owner** — full access to all settings, content, and billing. There is one Owner per workspace.
- **Admin** — full access to content and most settings, including user management.
- **Editor** — can create, edit, and publish articles and categories.
- **Draft Writer** — can create and edit articles but cannot publish them.
- **Viewer (internal)** — read-only access to the portal; cannot create or edit content.

For full details on built-in roles and how to create custom roles, see [Roles & Permissions](/settings/roles-permissions).

## Creating groups

Groups let you apply a role and content permissions to multiple users at once, saving you from updating each person individually when access requirements change.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Groups tab">
    Go to **Settings** → **Users & permissions** → **Users & groups** → **Groups** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new group">
    Click **New group** and enter a descriptive name (for example, "Technical Writers" or "Customer Success Team").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add members">
    Search for and add existing team members to the group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a role">
    Select the role that all members of this group will inherit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. All group members immediately receive the permissions associated with the assigned role.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can add individual users to multiple groups and adjust group membership at any time.

## Removing a user

To revoke a team member's portal access, find them in the **Users** list under **Users & groups**, click the options menu next to their name, and select **Revoke access**.

Revoking access immediately prevents the user from logging into the portal. All articles and contributions that the user created are retained in full — removing a user does not delete their content.

<Note>
  The number of team members you can invite depends on your Document360 plan. To check your current seat usage and available seats, go to **Settings** → **Billing**. If you need to add more seats, you can upgrade your plan from the same page.
</Note>
