The Billing section of Document360 gives you full visibility and control over your subscription — from the plan you are on and how many seats you are using, to your payment method and invoice history. Only Admins and Owners can access billing settings.

## Viewing your current plan

To see your subscription details, go to **Settings > Knowledge base portal > Billing**. The overview page shows:

- **Plan name** — the Document360 plan you are currently subscribed to.
- **Billing cycle** — whether you are billed monthly or annually.
- **Next renewal date** — the date your subscription renews and your next payment is due.
- **Included features** — a summary of the features and limits (articles, workspaces, team members, storage) included in your current plan.

## Upgrading or changing your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open plan options">
    From the Billing overview page, click **Upgrade plan**. A comparison of all available Document360 plans is displayed, showing features and pricing for each tier.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a new plan">
    Click **Select** on the plan you want to switch to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Review the pricing summary and click **Confirm**. For upgrades, the change takes effect immediately and you are charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current billing period. For downgrades, the change takes effect at the start of your next billing cycle — you retain your current plan's features until then.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing seats

Most Document360 plans are priced per team member seat. The Billing page shows your current seat usage — the number of seats in use versus the total seats included in your plan.

To add a new team member and consume a seat, go to **Settings > Users & permissions > Invite user**, enter the new member's email address, assign a role, and send the invitation. If your invitation would exceed your current seat limit, Document360 prompts you to add a seat before completing the invitation. Additional seats are billed at your plan's per-seat rate, prorated to your next renewal date.

To free up a seat, remove a team member from **Settings > Users & permissions**. The seat becomes available for a new invitee immediately.

## Updating your payment method

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open payment settings">
    Go to **Settings > Knowledge base portal > Billing > Payment method**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update">
    Click **Update**. Enter your new credit card details or update your billing address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The new payment method is used for all future charges.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Invoices and receipts

Document360 generates an invoice for every charge on your account. To view and download your invoice history:

1. Go to **Settings > Knowledge base portal > Billing > Invoice history**.
2. The table lists all past invoices with the date, amount, and payment status.
3. Click the **Download** icon next to any invoice to save it as a PDF. You can share these PDFs with your finance team for expense reporting or accounting purposes.

<Tip>
  For enterprise pricing, custom seat limits, volume discounts, or specific SLA requirements, contact the Document360 sales and support team directly. Enterprise agreements are handled outside the self-serve billing portal and can be tailored to your organization's needs.
</Tip>
