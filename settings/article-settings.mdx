Article settings let you fine-tune how individual articles are presented to your readers. You can switch features on or off globally across your entire knowledge base, choosing the combination that best fits your documentation style and your readers' needs.

## Location

Navigate to **Settings** → **Knowledge base site** → **Article settings** to access all the toggles described below. Changes apply immediately to all articles across your knowledge base.

## Available settings

<Accordion title="Reading time">
  When enabled, an estimated reading time appears at the top of each article — for example, "4 min read." Document360 calculates this estimate based on the article's word count using an average adult reading speed of approximately 200 words per minute. This helps readers gauge how long an article will take before they commit to reading it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Article comments">
  Enabling comments allows readers to leave feedback directly on individual articles. You can configure whether commenting requires a reader account login or allows anonymous submissions. Comments are moderated from the Document360 portal under the **Discussions** section, where your team can reply, resolve, or delete threads.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Related articles">
  When enabled, a **You may also like** section appears at the bottom of each article. Document360 uses AI to surface related articles based on content similarity and reader behavior. This helps readers discover relevant content they might not have searched for directly.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Table of contents">
  Enabling the table of contents automatically generates a floating panel on the right side of each article page. The TOC is built from **H2** and **H3** headings within the article and updates dynamically as readers scroll. Readers can click any heading in the TOC to jump directly to that section. This setting complements the global layout toggle in Site customization.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Glossary term highlighting">
  When enabled, any term that exists in your Document360 glossary is automatically underlined in article body text. Readers can hover over the underlined term to see a tooltip showing the term's definition — without leaving the article. This is especially useful for technical documentation with specialized terminology.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Print button">
  Adds a **Print** button to each article page. Readers can click it to open the browser's print dialog, which also supports saving the article as a PDF. The print view strips navigation elements and formats the article cleanly for printed output.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Breadcrumbs">
  When enabled, a breadcrumb trail appears above the article title showing the full category path — for example, **Getting Started** → **Installation** → **System Requirements**. Breadcrumbs help readers understand where an article sits in your content hierarchy and provide a quick way to navigate up to parent categories.
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  Enable **Related articles** to reduce reader bounce rates and help customers self-serve more effectively. When readers find answers to follow-up questions without leaving your documentation, they are less likely to submit a support ticket.
</Tip>
