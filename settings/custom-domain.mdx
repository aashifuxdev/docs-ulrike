By default, your Document360 knowledge base is hosted on a `*.document360.io` subdomain. Mapping a custom domain — such as `docs.yourcompany.com` — gives your readers a fully branded URL and reinforces trust. Document360 also automatically provisions an SSL certificate so your site is served securely over HTTPS.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- **Access to your domain's DNS settings** through your domain registrar (for example, GoDaddy, Namecheap, Cloudflare, or Route 53). You need permission to create CNAME records.
- **A subdomain in mind** — Document360 supports subdomains (like `docs.yourcompany.com` or `help.yourcompany.com`). Root apex domains (like `yourcompany.com`) require additional configuration at your registrar and are not recommended.

Document360 works with any domain registrar that supports CNAME records.

## Adding the custom domain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the custom domain settings">
    Go to **Settings** → **Knowledge base site** → **Custom domain**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your domain">
    Click **Add domain** and enter the full subdomain you want to use (for example, `docs.yourcompany.com`), then click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the DNS records">
    Document360 displays the DNS records you need to add at your registrar. Keep this page open — you'll need these values in the next section.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Configuring DNS

Log in to your domain registrar's DNS management console and create a CNAME record using the values provided by Document360. A typical record looks like this:

```text
Type: CNAME
Name: docs
Value: custom.document360.io
TTL: 3600
```

- **Name** is the subdomain prefix (just `docs`, not the full `docs.yourcompany.com`).
- **Value** is the Document360 endpoint shown on your custom domain settings page — copy it exactly.
- **TTL** of 3600 seconds (one hour) is a safe default; lower values speed up propagation but increase DNS query load.

Save your DNS record and allow time for it to propagate across the internet.

## SSL certificate

Once Document360 detects that your DNS record is pointing correctly, it automatically provisions a free **Let's Encrypt SSL certificate** for your custom domain. You do not need to purchase or upload a certificate manually.

SSL provisioning typically completes within **15–60 minutes** of successful DNS propagation. Until it completes, your custom domain may show a browser security warning — this is normal and resolves on its own.

## Verifying your domain

Return to **Settings** → **Knowledge base site** → **Custom domain** to check your domain status.

- **Pending** — DNS has not yet propagated or Document360 has not detected the CNAME record.
- **Active** — DNS is confirmed and your knowledge base is live on your custom domain.

Click **Check status** at any time to trigger an immediate re-check rather than waiting for the next automated check.

<Note>
  Changing your custom domain does not automatically redirect readers from the old URL to the new one. If you previously shared your `*.document360.io` URL with readers, set up redirect rules under **Settings** → **Knowledge base site** → **Redirects** to send traffic from the old address to the new domain.
</Note>
