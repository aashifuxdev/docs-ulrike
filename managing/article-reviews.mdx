Documentation that isn't maintained quickly becomes a liability. Outdated articles erode reader trust, generate support tickets, and create confusion for new users. Document360's article review reminder system lets you schedule recurring prompts for any article so that the right team member is notified when content needs a fresh look — before readers notice it's stale.

<Tip>
  Set a **30-day review cycle** for rapidly changing content such as API reference docs or release notes, and a **180-day cycle** for stable content like conceptual overviews or company policies. Matching the review frequency to the content's rate of change keeps your workload manageable without letting anything slip through the cracks.
</Tip>

## Setting a review reminder on an article

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the article">
    Open the article you want to schedule a review for in the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate Review reminder in the right panel">
    In the right-hand panel, scroll down to the **Review reminder** section and toggle it **on**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the review frequency">
    Choose how often the reminder should trigger: **30**, **60**, **90**, or **180** days from the last review date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a team member">
    Select the team member responsible for reviewing this article. They will receive an email notification when the reminder is due.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** (or publish your changes) to activate the reminder. The reminder clock starts from the moment you save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing review reminders in bulk

To get a bird's-eye view of all review reminders across your knowledge base, go to **Content tools → Article review reminders**. This dashboard shows:

- Every article that has an active reminder
- The **due date** for each upcoming review
- The **assigned reviewer** for each article

From this view you can filter reminders by due date, category, or assignee to prioritize work. You can also update the frequency or assignee for multiple articles at once, or dismiss reminders for articles that have already been reviewed outside the system.

## What happens when a review is due

When a review reminder reaches its due date, two things happen automatically:

1. **Email notification** — the assigned team member receives an email with a direct link to the article, prompting them to review and update it.
2. **Review due indicator** — the article is flagged with a **Review due** badge in the Documentation panel, making it immediately visible to anyone browsing the category tree.

Once the reviewer updates the article, they can reset the reminder from the right panel — either keeping the same frequency or adjusting it before saving the new review date.

## Workflow integration

If the **Workflow designer** is enabled for your portal (go to **Settings → Knowledge base portal** to check), you can connect review reminders to your custom publishing workflow. When a reminder triggers, it can automatically create a workflow task and assign it to a specific role — such as a technical writer or subject-matter expert — so the review process follows the same approval steps as new content creation. This is especially useful for teams that require peer review or manager sign-off before an updated article is republished.
