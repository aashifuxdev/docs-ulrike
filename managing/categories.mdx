Categories are the backbone of your knowledge base. They group related articles into a logical hierarchy that helps readers navigate your content and find answers quickly. Document360 gives you full control over your category structure — you can create, nest, rename, reorder, and delete categories at any time without disrupting published content.

<Tip>
  Use top-level categories as major product areas — for example, **Getting Started**, **Integrations**, and **Troubleshooting**. This gives readers an immediate mental map of your knowledge base before they dive into specific articles.
</Tip>

## Creating a category

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Documentation panel">
    In the left-hand Documentation panel, locate the category tree.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the + icon">
    Click the **+** icon at the top of the tree to create a top-level category, or click the **+** icon that appears next to an existing category to create a subcategory beneath it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Category">
    From the dropdown that appears, select **Category**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a name">
    Type your category name in the inline field that appears, then press **Enter** to save it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Nesting categories

Document360 supports up to **6 levels of nesting**, allowing you to model complex content hierarchies. To nest a category, drag it from its current position in the tree and drop it on top of the category you want to make its parent. A visual indicator appears when the drop target is valid.

<Warning>
  Deep nesting can make navigation harder for readers. As a best practice, keep your hierarchy to **3 levels or fewer** — a top-level category, a subcategory, and articles beneath it. Reserve deeper nesting for large knowledge bases with genuinely distinct sub-topics.
</Warning>

## Reordering categories

To change the order in which categories appear, drag a category up or down within the same level of the tree and drop it in the desired position. The order you set here is reflected directly in your public knowledge base site's navigation menu, so arrange categories in the sequence that makes the most sense for your readers.

## Category settings

Right-click any category in the tree — or click the **⋮** (more options) menu that appears on hover — to access its settings. From this menu you can:

- **Rename** — update the display name of the category.
- **Set slug** — customize the URL-friendly identifier used in the category's public URL path.
- **Add description** — write a short description that appears on the category landing page.
- **Set SEO metadata** — configure the SEO title and meta description for the category page (see [Tags & SEO](/managing/tags-seo) for details).
- **Set visibility** — toggle the category between **Public** (visible to all readers) and **Private** (visible only to logged-in team members or specific reader groups).
- **Move** — relocate the category under a different parent without drag-and-drop.

## Deleting a category

You can delete a category from its **⋮** menu. However, take care before doing so.

<Warning>
  Deleting a category **permanently deletes all articles inside it**, including any nested subcategories and their articles. This action cannot be undone. Before deleting, use the **Move** option on each article or subcategory to relocate them to another category so no content is lost.
</Warning>
