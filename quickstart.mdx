---

This quickstart walks you through everything you need to go from a blank slate to a live, public-facing knowledge base. By the end, you will have a Document360 project set up, your first category and article created, and a shareable URL ready to send to your users.

<Note>
  Document360 offers a free trial with no credit card required. You can explore every feature before committing to a plan.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up and create your project">
    Go to [app.document360.com](https://app.document360.com) and click **Get started free**. Enter your name, work email address, and a password, then confirm your email when the verification message arrives.

    Once you are inside the app, Document360 prompts you to create your first project. Give your project a descriptive name — for example, `Acme Help Center` — and choose a workspace name such as `Production`. The workspace acts as an isolated environment within your project, which is useful later when you want separate staging and production spaces or multiple product lines under one account.

    Click **Create project** to finish. Document360 provisions your knowledge base and drops you into the main editor view.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your first category">
    In the left sidebar, click **Documentation** to open the content tree. The tree is where you organize all of your categories and articles in a nested hierarchy.

    Click the **+** button at the top of the tree and select **Add category**. Type a name for the category — for example, `Getting Started` — and press **Enter**. Document360 creates the category and adds it to the tree immediately.

    You can nest categories inside each other by dragging them, or by repeating this step and choosing a parent when prompted. Keep your top-level categories broad so readers can navigate quickly.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write your first article">
    Click the category you just created in the content tree. Then click **New Article** in the upper-right area of the tree panel. Document360 opens a blank article inside the block editor.

    Type a title in the **Article title** field at the top. In the editor body below, click anywhere and start typing your content. The block editor works like a modern document tool — press `/` to open the block menu and insert headings, bullet lists, code blocks, images, callouts, and more.

    To speed up your writing, click the **Eddy AI** button in the editor toolbar. Eddy AI can generate a first draft from a prompt, expand a sentence into a full paragraph, rewrite text in a different tone, or summarize a long section — all without leaving the editor.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Publish your knowledge base">
    When your article is ready, click the **Publish** button in the top-right corner of the editor. The article status changes from **Draft** to **Published** and becomes visible to anyone with access to your knowledge base site.

    To find your public URL, go to **Settings → Knowledge base site** in the left sidebar. Your site URL is displayed at the top of that page. You can share this link directly, map it to a custom domain, or embed a search widget into your existing product from this same settings area.
  </Step>
</Steps>
