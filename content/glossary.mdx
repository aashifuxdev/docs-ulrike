The Document360 glossary lets you define the technical terms, acronyms, and product-specific language used throughout your knowledge base. Once a term is defined, it appears with a dotted underline wherever it occurs in your articles, and readers can hover over it to see the definition in a tooltip — without ever leaving the page.

<Tip>
  Keep definitions concise — ideally under 160 characters. Longer definitions get clipped in the tooltip and are harder to read at a glance. Save detailed explanations for a dedicated article you can link to from the definition.
</Tip>

## Adding a term

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Glossary">
    In the left sidebar, click **Content tools**, then select **Glossary**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new term">
    Click **New term** in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the term details">
    Fill in the **Term name** — exactly as it should appear on the site. Add a **Short definition** (shown in the tooltip) and an optional **Extended description** for readers who want more context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the term">
    Click **Save**. The term is now part of your glossary and is eligible for automatic highlighting in articles.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once saved, any occurrence of the term in your published articles appears with a dotted underline. Hovering over the underlined word shows the short definition in a tooltip directly on the page.

## Enabling automatic term highlighting

By default, glossary terms are highlighted automatically across all articles. You can control this behaviour in **Settings > Knowledge base site > Article settings**. Toggle **Automatic glossary highlighting** on or off to enable or disable the feature workspace-wide. When the toggle is off, terms are still stored in your glossary but do not receive visual highlighting in articles.

## Editing and organizing terms

To manage your glossary, go to **Content tools > Glossary**. The list page shows all your terms sorted alphabetically. Use the search bar at the top to find a specific term, or scroll through the alphabetical index on the left to jump to a letter group.

Click any term name to open it and edit the definition inline. Changes take effect on the published site after you save. To delete a term, click the **⋮** menu on the term's row and select **Delete**. Deleting a term removes its tooltip from the site but does not alter the article text itself.

## Supported content in definitions

The **Short definition** and **Extended description** fields support basic rich text formatting: **bold**, *italic*, and hyperlinks. This lets you emphasise key parts of a definition or link to a related article for deeper reading.

Definitions do not support block-level content such as images, code blocks, tables, or callout boxes. If you need to include structured content alongside a term, create a dedicated article and link to it from the extended description.
