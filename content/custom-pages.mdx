Custom pages are standalone pages you build inside Document360 that exist independently of your category tree. Unlike regular articles, custom pages are not assigned to a category or section — they live at their own URL and serve purposes that fall outside the scope of structured documentation, such as a changelog, a privacy policy, a landing page, or a contact page.

<Tip>
  Common uses for custom pages include: release notes and changelogs, privacy policy and terms of service, contact or support request pages, API status pages, and product landing pages.
</Tip>

## Creating a custom page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Custom pages">
    In the left sidebar, click **Content tools**, then select **Custom pages**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new page">
    Click **New page** in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the title and slug">
    Enter a **Page title** — this appears as the page heading and browser tab title. Set a **Slug** to define the page's URL path, for example `changelog` or `privacy-policy`. The slug must be unique within your knowledge base.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the page content">
    Use the full block editor to build your page. All block types available in the article editor are available here, including headings, tables, code blocks, media, callouts, and accordions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and publish">
    Click **Save** to store your draft, then click **Publish** to make the page live on your knowledge base site.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where custom pages appear

Custom pages appear in a dedicated section of your public knowledge base navigation, separate from the main category tree. Readers can navigate to them via the header or footer links you configure, or through direct links you place in articles or external communications. Custom pages do not appear in the standard article sidebar navigation by default.

## Custom page vs. Article

Use this table to decide whether to create a custom page or a standard article.

| Feature | Article | Custom Page |
|---|---|---|
| Lives in category tree | Yes | No |
| Appears in search | Yes | Configurable |
| Can use templates | Yes | No |
| Supports all block types | Yes | Yes |

## Adding custom pages to navigation

To surface a custom page in your site's header or footer, go to **Settings > Knowledge base site > Navigation**. From there, you can add links pointing to your custom page's slug in either the header navigation bar or the footer link section. This makes the page discoverable to all readers without requiring them to know its direct URL.
