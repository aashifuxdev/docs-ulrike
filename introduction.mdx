Document360 is a knowledge base platform designed for teams that need to create, manage, and publish documentation at scale. Whether you're building a self-service customer portal, technical API reference, or an internal knowledge hub, Document360 gives you the tools to write faster, organize smarter, and deliver a great reading experience — all without writing a line of code.

## Key capabilities

- **Eddy AI** — Generate article drafts, improve existing content, and power an AI chatbot on your knowledge base site with a single toggle.
- **Multi-workspace & multi-language** — Maintain separate workspaces for different products or audiences, each with independent language versions.
- **Rich content editor** — Format articles with the block editor, embed code snippets, add videos, create tables, and reuse content with snippets and variables.
- **Analytics & feedback** — See which articles get the most traffic, where readers drop off, and collect direct article feedback to prioritize improvements.
- **Customizable portal** — Apply your brand colors, fonts, and custom domain to the public-facing site.
- **Team collaboration** — Assign roles, set review workflows, and audit every change your team makes.
