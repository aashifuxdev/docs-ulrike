The Eddy AI chatbot gives your readers instant, conversational answers directly on your public knowledge base site — without them having to search or scroll through articles. Powered by your published content and any custom knowledge sources you add, the chatbot reduces support load and helps readers find solutions faster, any time of day.

## Enabling the chatbot

The chatbot has its own toggle that is separate from the master Eddy AI switch. Make sure Eddy AI is already enabled in **Settings > AI settings > Eddy AI settings** before proceeding.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Eddy AI settings">
    In the portal, navigate to **Settings** > **AI settings** > **Eddy AI settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll to the Chatbot section">
    Within Eddy AI settings, scroll down to the **Chatbot** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the chatbot">
    Toggle **Enable Eddy AI chatbot** to the on position.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After you enable it, a floating chat button appears on your public knowledge base site. Readers click the button to open the chat panel and start asking questions.

## How the chatbot answers questions

When a reader submits a question, Eddy AI searches across all of your **published articles** and any **custom sources** you've added in [Manage sources](/ai/manage-sources). It synthesizes the most relevant information and returns a concise, natural-language answer. Each response includes **citations with links** back to the source articles or documents, so readers can easily read more or verify the information themselves.

The chatbot understands follow-up questions within the same conversation thread, making it possible for readers to have a multi-turn dialogue rather than starting over with each new query.

## Customizing the chatbot

You can adjust the chatbot's appearance and behavior to match your brand and audience.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the customization panel">
    Go to **Settings** > **AI settings** > **Customize Eddy AI**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the bot name and avatar">
    Enter a **Bot name** (for example, your brand name or "Support Assistant") and upload a custom **avatar icon** that appears in the chat header and the floating button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write a greeting message">
    Enter a **Greeting message** — the first message readers see when they open the chat panel. Use it to set expectations or invite a question.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add suggested questions">
    Enter up to five **Suggested questions** that appear as clickable prompts when the chat opens. These help readers get started immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the chatbot language">
    Choose the default **Language** for the chatbot interface labels and responses. The chatbot can also detect the reader's browser language if you enable multilingual mode.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Add two or three suggested starter questions in **Customize Eddy AI** that reflect your most common support topics. Readers who aren't sure what to ask are much more likely to engage when they see a ready-made prompt they can click.
</Tip>

## Chatbot on the KB widget

Eddy AI chat is also available inside the **embedded knowledge base widget** — the inline or pop-up widget you can embed on your product or website. To enable it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to widget settings">
    Navigate to **Connections** > **Knowledge base widget settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Eddy AI in the widget">
    Find the **Eddy AI** toggle within the widget configuration and turn it on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and republish the widget">
    Save your changes. The chat tab appears automatically in the widget for your site visitors.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reviewing chatbot conversations

Understanding how readers interact with the chatbot helps you identify documentation gaps and improve your content over time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Feedback manager">
    Go to **Connections** > **Feedback manager** in the portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter for chatbot conversations">
    Select the **Chatbot** tab to view conversation threads from your readers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review feedback and unanswered questions">
    Check thumbs-up and thumbs-down ratings on individual responses. Pay particular attention to questions the chatbot marked as **unanswered** — these indicate topics your knowledge base doesn't yet cover.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use unanswered questions and low-rated responses as a prioritized content backlog. Writing articles on those topics will improve chatbot accuracy and reduce support tickets.

<Note>
  The Eddy AI chatbot only answers questions based on your published articles and the custom sources you've added in Manage sources. It does not access the internet or any external information that you haven't explicitly provided.
</Note>
