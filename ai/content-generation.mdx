Eddy AI brings AI-assisted writing directly into the Document360 editor, so you can draft, refine, and translate content without ever leaving the page you're working on. Whether you're starting from a blank canvas or polishing a rough draft, Eddy AI accelerates every stage of the writing process while keeping you in full control of the final output.

## Generating a new article draft

When you have a topic in mind but want a head start, Eddy AI can produce a complete first draft in seconds.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a new article">
    In the portal, open the project and click **New article**. Give the article a title and open it in the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Eddy AI in the editor">
    Click inside the empty article body. Then either click the **Eddy AI icon** (sparkle ✦) in the editor toolbar, or press **/** to open the slash command menu and select **Eddy AI**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Generate article">
    From the Eddy AI menu, choose **Generate article**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a topic description">
    In the prompt field, type a short description of the article, a list of key points to cover, or both. The more specific your input, the better the output.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Generate">
    Click **Generate**. Eddy AI produces a full draft — including headings, body paragraphs, and suggested structure — which is inserted into the editor. Review and edit the draft as needed before publishing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use bullet points as your input rather than a single vague sentence. For example, list the specific steps, concepts, or outcomes the article should cover. Eddy AI produces better-structured, more accurate drafts from a clear list of key points than from an open-ended prompt.
</Tip>

## Improving existing content

You can run Eddy AI improvement actions on any selected text in the editor at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the text you want to improve">
    Highlight any passage — a sentence, a paragraph, or a larger section — in the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Eddy AI action menu">
    Click the **Eddy AI icon** that appears above or beside the selection (or right-click and choose **Eddy AI**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an improvement action">
    Select one of the following options:

    - **Improve writing** — Enhances overall clarity, flow, and quality without changing the meaning.
    - **Expand** — Adds more detail, context, or examples to flesh out the selected passage.
    - **Shorten** — Makes the passage more concise by removing unnecessary words and redundancy.
    - **Fix grammar** — Corrects grammatical errors, punctuation, and spelling.
    - **Change tone** — Rewrites the passage in a different tone: **Professional**, **Casual**, or **Friendly**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and accept the suggestion">
    Eddy AI displays the revised text alongside the original. Click **Replace** to accept the change, **Try again** to generate a new variation, or **Discard** to keep your original text.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Translating articles

Eddy AI can automatically translate an article into any language you have enabled in your project, dramatically speeding up your localization workflow.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the article you want to translate">
    Navigate to the article in the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the target language">
    In the **language selector** at the top of the editor (or in the article's localization panel), choose the target language you want to translate into.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Translate with Eddy AI">
    Click **Translate with Eddy AI**. Eddy AI fills in the translated version of the article body automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and publish">
    Review the translated content, make any necessary edits for accuracy or terminology, and then publish the translated version when it's ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Generating SEO meta descriptions

Eddy AI can write an optimized meta description for each article based on its content, saving you time and improving search discoverability.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the SEO panel">
    With your article open in the editor, expand the **SEO** panel in the right-hand settings sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the meta description">
    Next to the **Meta description** field, click the **Auto-generate** button (the Eddy AI sparkle icon). Eddy AI reads your article content and generates a concise, keyword-relevant description optimized for search engines.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and save">
    Edit the generated description if needed — aim for 150–160 characters — then save the article.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Eddy AI writing features are available in the editor only when Eddy AI is enabled at the project level. If you don't see the Eddy AI icon or options, go to **Settings** > **AI settings** > **Eddy AI settings** and make sure the **Enable Eddy AI** toggle is turned on.
</Note>
