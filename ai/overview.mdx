Eddy AI is Document360's built-in artificial intelligence assistant, designed to help your team write better documentation faster and give your readers instant, accurate answers without leaving your knowledge base site. Whether you're drafting a new article from scratch, polishing existing content, translating into multiple languages, or deploying a 24/7 chatbot, Eddy AI handles the heavy lifting so you can focus on what matters most.

## Enabling Eddy AI

Before using any Eddy AI feature, you need to turn on the master toggle in your project settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI settings">
    In the Document360 portal, go to **Settings** > **AI settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Eddy AI settings">
    Select **Eddy AI settings** from the left panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Eddy AI">
    Toggle **Enable Eddy AI** to the on position and confirm when prompted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once the master toggle is on, writing-assistance features (generation, improvement, translation) become available immediately in the editor. Some features have their own additional toggles — for example, the AI chatbot has a separate **Enable Eddy AI chatbot** switch within the same settings area.

## Eddy AI and your data

When you use Eddy AI, the content you submit — such as article text, prompts, or uploaded files — is sent to the AI service to generate a response. This content is used solely to produce the output you requested and is **not** used to train shared AI models or made available to other customers. Review the [Document360 Privacy Policy](https://document360.com/privacy-policy/) for full details on data handling and retention.
