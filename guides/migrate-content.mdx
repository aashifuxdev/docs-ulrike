Moving your existing documentation into Document360 is straightforward with the built-in migration tools. Whether you're coming from a dedicated help center platform, a wiki, or a custom website, Document360 provides guided workflows to bring your content across with minimal manual effort.

## Supported migration sources

Document360 supports direct migration from the following platforms:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Zendesk Guide" icon="z">
    Import your Zendesk help center articles, categories, and sections directly using your Zendesk credentials.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Freshdesk Solutions" icon="f">
    Connect your Freshdesk account to pull in solution articles and folder structures.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Confluence" icon="c">
    Migrate Confluence spaces and pages, preserving your page hierarchy where possible.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Intercom Articles" icon="comment">
    Import your Intercom help center articles and collections into Document360.
  </Card>
  <Card title="GitBook" icon="book">
    Bring in GitBook spaces, including nested pages and code blocks.
  </Card>
  <Card title="WordPress" icon="w">
    Migrate WordPress posts and pages, converting them to Document360 articles.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Generic HTML/Markdown" icon="file-code">
    Upload exported HTML or Markdown files from any other source tool.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Using the Migrate content tool

The Migrate content tool walks you through a step-by-step process to connect your source platform and import your articles.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open migration settings">
    Go to **Settings** → **Knowledge base portal** → **Migrate content**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your source platform">
    Choose your source platform from the list of supported integrations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your source account or upload files">
    Depending on the platform, either authenticate with your source account credentials or upload the exported files (for example, a ZIP archive of HTML or Markdown files).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map categories">
    Document360 displays your source categories and sections. Map each one to an existing or new category in your Document360 workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review content">
    Preview the articles that will be imported. Deselect any content you do not want to bring across.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the migration">
    Click **Migrate** to start the import. Document360 imports all selected content as **draft articles** so you can review and edit each one before publishing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

All migrated articles land in Draft status, giving you full control over what goes live and when.

## After migration

Once the migration completes, take the following steps to prepare your content for publication:

- **Review article formatting** — Check that headings, tables, images, and code blocks have transferred correctly. Some rich-text elements may need minor clean-up depending on the source platform.
- **Add tags and SEO metadata** — Open each article, navigate to the **SEO** tab, and add a meta title, meta description, and relevant tags to improve discoverability.
- **Set up redirects from old URLs** — To avoid broken links, go to **Settings** → **Knowledge base site** → **Article redirect rules** and create redirect mappings from your old article URLs to their new Document360 URLs.
- **Publish your articles** — When you are satisfied with each article, change its status from Draft to Published to make it visible on your knowledge base site.

<Tip>
  Use **Eddy AI** to speed up post-migration clean-up. Open any imported article, select the Eddy AI assistant, and prompt it to reformat, summarize, or improve the content to match your style guide.
</Tip>

## Manual import

If your source tool is not listed in the Migrate content section, you can still import your content manually.

Export your content from the source tool as Markdown (`.md`) or HTML (`.html`) files, then follow these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Import and export">
    Go to **Content tools** → **Import & export project** → **Import**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your files">
    Upload your exported Markdown or HTML files. You can upload individual files or a ZIP archive containing multiple files.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map and review">
    Assign the imported articles to the appropriate categories in your workspace, then review the content before publishing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Migration is workspace-specific. If you manage multiple workspaces in Document360, you must run a separate migration for each workspace — content does not carry over between workspaces automatically.
</Note>
